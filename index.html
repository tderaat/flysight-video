Disclaimer: Your jump files, notes and settings never leave this device: nothing is uploaded to a server. Everything is kept in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Clearing site data or browsing history, a private window, switching browser or device, or the browser freeing up space can delete it without warning. Keep your original CSV files as your backup.
